Order Flow

position changes since last poll
NEW is a position that did not exist last poll. ADD and TRIM are size changes. EXIT is a full close, confirmed against Polymarket and ignored when the market has simply settled. A trader who just joined the leaderboard is used as a baseline first, so their existing book is not reported as new.

Every public trade at or above the size you pick, from any wallet, newest first. BUY and SELL are that wallet's side of the trade. A trader marked top is on a tracked leaderboard. Prints trail the live book by a few seconds.

Heatmap

the most-traded live markets at a glance
Each tile is one market. Its area is the measure you pick and its color is how far the first outcome's price has moved. Hover a tile for the numbers. Click it for the chart, the order book and the holders. Tiles are grouped by category, largest first.
